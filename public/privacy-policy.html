  • Advertising data. Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / Apple IDFA where you permit it), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data, processed by our advertising partners.
  • Purchase data. Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We receive purchase receipts/transaction identifiers, not your payment card details.
  • Support communications. Anything you send us when you contact support.

We do not knowingly collect precise location, contacts, photos or microphone data.

  • To respond to support requests and to comply with legal obligations.
